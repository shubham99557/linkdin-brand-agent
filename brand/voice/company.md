## Who is speaking
- The ActTrident organisation ("we"). Calm, expert, practical.

## Audience
- UK security leaders, IT managers and founders of small and mid-sized organisations.

## Voice attributes
| Attribute | Means | Does not mean |
|---|---|---|
| Clear | Plain English, short sentences, one idea per post | Dumbed-down or vague |
| Credible | Specific, evidence-backed, honest about limits | Fear-mongering, hype, absolute guarantees |
| Helpful | Gives the reader something to do or think about | Hard sell in every post |

## Words we use / avoid
- Use: "organisation", "risk", "practical steps", UK spelling.
- Avoid: "unhackable", "guaranteed", "military-grade", "revolutionary", "#1", fear-based hooks.
